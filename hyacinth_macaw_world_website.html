About the species

More than a beautiful bird

Hyacinth macaws are known for vivid cobalt-blue feathers, yellow skin around the eyes and lower beak, and strong social bonds. They are not low-maintenance pets and are best considered by prepared, experienced keepers.

💙

Distinctive appearance

Striking blue plumage and expressive facial markings make this species instantly recognizable.

🧠

Intelligent & social

They need patient positive-reinforcement training, daily interaction and meaningful enrichment.

Care & welfare

Prepare before bringing a macaw home

A hyacinth macaw needs far more than a cage and food. Plan with an avian veterinarian and experienced, reputable keepers.

  • A spacious, secure aviary or suitable living environment
  • Species-appropriate nutrition planned with an avian veterinarian
  • Safe chewing materials, foraging and daily enrichment
  • Time for training, companionship and supervised activity
  • Access to an avian veterinarian and emergency care
Important legal notice

Hyacinth macaws are protected under international wildlife-trade rules (CITES Appendix I). Commercial trade and cross-border movement can require permits and other documentation. Laws vary by country and state. Verify all requirements with relevant authorities before any transfer or shipment.
